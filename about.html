Our Journey

Evolution of Excellence

2022

The Kick-Off

SkillSkill was founded by a collective of tactical analysts and former e-football pros. Our mission started with breaking down complex game mechanics into digestible, elite-tier learning modules.

Global Expansion

We opened access to over 50 countries and launched our first "Master Class" series. SkillSkill became recognized as the fastest-growing elite gaming community across Asia and Europe.

Forging strategic partnerships with major football clubs, we began integrating real-world statistical data into in-game strategies. Our active membership surpassed 20,000 players.

Today, SkillSkill is the global gold standard for e-football coaching. Utilizing AI-driven analysis, we empower every player to dominate the pitch and reach their ultimate form.
